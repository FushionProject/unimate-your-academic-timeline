How photosynthesis stores solar energy

Chlorophyll absorbs light energy and helps convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose.

Oxygen is released as a byproduct during the light-dependent reactions.

In the Calvin cycle, plants use chemical energy carried by ATP and NADPH to assemble carbohydrate molecules. This process supports growth while linking atmospheric carbon to the organic compounds used throughout an ecosystem.
