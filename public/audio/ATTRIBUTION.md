# Study audio sources

All study-player tracks are bundled with UniMate, so playback does not depend on a third-party stream. Source files were converted to MP3 for browser compatibility. Ambient recordings were trimmed where noted and are looped by the player.

- **Lofi Hip Hop** — “Perspective” by Sappheiros, licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). [Source](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sappheiros_-_Perspective_(Lofi_Hip_Hop).ogg>).
- **Rain** — “Rain against the Window” by cori, released into the public domain. Trimmed to 80 seconds. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_against_the_window.ogg).
- **Ocean** — “On a pebble beach” by earthcalling, released into the public domain. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:On_a_pebble_beach.ogg).
- **Coffee Shop** — “Cafe ambiance” by Marble Toast, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Trimmed to 120 seconds. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cafe_ambiance.ogg).
- **Classical** — J. S. Bach’s “Air on the G String,” performed by Joel Belov with Robert Gayler (1920 recording), public domain. [Source](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Air_(Bach).ogg>).
